6G Technology

Introduction

6G technology is the sixth generation of wireless communication that is expected to succeed 5G. It aims to provide ultra-fast internet speed, low latency, and advanced connectivity.

Key Features

6G is expected to offer speeds up to 1 Tbps, extremely low latency, and better energy efficiency compared to previous generations.

Applications

It will support advanced technologies such as holographic communication, smart cities, autonomous vehicles, and remote surgeries.

Advantages

6G will improve global connectivity, enhance artificial intelligence integration, and provide seamless communication between devices.
